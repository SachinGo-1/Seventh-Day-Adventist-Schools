What is a Healthcare Administration Professional?

Healthcare administration professionals have responsibilities that are entirely different from the responsibilities of a doctor or physician. While physicians manage patients directly, healthcare administrators oversee the facility itself.

What Does a Healthcare Administration Professional Do?

Healthcare Administrative Professionals play a pivotal role in the smooth operation of healthcare facilities. They oversee daily operations, manage billing and insurance claims, and ensure compliance with healthcare regulations.

Our Healthcare Administrative Professional certificate program equips you with the essential skills to excel in this field. You'll gain a solid understanding of medical terminology, record-keeping best practices, and healthcare compliance. Additionally, you'll learn how to perform front office procedures and manage health records effectively.

In This Program You Will:

  • Understand regulatory laws and compliance
  • Learn how to monitor and audit electronic health records
  • Recognize and use appropriate billing codes
  • Learn front of office procedures and organization

Requirements:

  • High School Diploma or GED
  • Web browser with internet connection
  • Course registration & payment
  • Oral and written English proficiency is a requirement for course participation and certificate examination

Program Materials Provided:

  • Expert-led video-based training
  • eBooks: Kinn’s Medical Administrative Assistant, Integrated Electronic Health Records & Medical Insurance, A Revenue Cycle Process Approach
  • Competency assessments
  • National Certification exam fees

What Can You Do with a Healthcare Administration Professional Certificate?

Medical support staff are indispensable for the proper management of medical establishments, encompassing acute care centers, physician practices, and non-inpatient treatment sites. Our program fully prepares you to step into the role of a Healthcare Administration Professional to contribute to the management and operational framework of various medical entities.
